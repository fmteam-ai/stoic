# Plugin catalog search (claude.ai plugin directory, 17 Oct 2026)

Search keywords: trading, market data, backtest, forex, gold, risk management, financial news. All are community-tier plugins from the Anthropic Directory unless noted; none were enabled. Descriptions below are the publishers' own claims and have not been verified.

- **QuanterLab**: backtests trading rules, runs walk-forward checks, scans for candidates, runs saved canvas strategies, and has published studies. Relevant to overfitting control (walk-forward), which complements TextToQuant (crypto-only free tier, 2 credits left).
- **TradeOS AI**: MCP for agentic technical analysis of XAUUSD, stocks, crypto and forex, with multi-timeframe charts, macro context and 24/7 smart alerts. It is a gold-specific second-opinion and alerts tool, not an execution tool.
- **Ultralayer**: realtime market intelligence (news, events, guidance, sentiment, alerts) with "point-in-time safety for backtests". A candidate news/event feed for the LLM reader and for backtests without look-ahead.
- **TickerLayer**: latest prices, history and market hours for crypto, forex, commodities, indices and bond yields. Could cross-check prices and yields; it partly overlaps with FMP and Alpha Vantage.
- **TradingCalc**: deterministic trading math (position size, pip value, VaR, Sharpe). Useful for verifying the risk math in reports.
- **Data** (Anthropic, official): SQL, analysis, dashboards and statistical-analysis skills. Useful for analysing trade logs and running A/B statistics on shadow tests.
- **Blocksize Market Data**: read-only market data, crypto-focused; less relevant now that BTC has been removed.
- **spera**: builds and backtests strategies on the Spera platform; it is platform-specific.

Connectors already available in this session: Alpha Vantage MCP, FMP MCP (free plan; calendar blocked), Bigdata.com MCP (news and research search with sentiment), TextToQuant MCP (backtests: crypto only, ≥1h timeframes, 2 lifetime credits left), Gmail, GitHub.
